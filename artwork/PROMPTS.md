## conan

Use case: stylized-concept. One landscape 16:9 background scene for both the title and match screen of a desktop card simulator. Place signature details on left, right and the skyline. Keep the central and lower board area simple with convincing material detail, functional overlay contrast and readable midtones. No dark filter, near-black vignette, UI or cards. Full bleed, professional original atmospheric artwork.

An original environmental interpretation of the street in fictional Beika where the Mouri Detective Agency sits above the Cafe Poirot in a narrow two-story corner building. Main identifying composition: on the upper left a detective-office window facing the street, with a warm desk lamp and a silhouetted louver, directly above a welcoming ground-floor coffee shop with a green fabric awning and brick doorway. Sign on the upstairs glass in accurately typeset simple Japanese '毛利探偵事務所', sign under the awning '喫茶ポアロ'. The physical building and camera angle are newly designed: modern Japanese two story brick-and-cream corner architecture, seen from across a reflective intersection with the low sun between buildings. A brighter honey/cobalt golden-hour investigation scene, Japanese illustrated mystery atmosphere, crisp daylight on the pavement. No characters, no logo treatment, no tracing or reproduction of an existing anime frame, game level or artwork.
